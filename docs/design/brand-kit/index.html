Dibs

Call dibs on what you ate. Split the bill. Pay by UPI. A bill splitter for friends in India.

Everyone pays for exactly what they ate. — the one rule every screen is checked against
01
Logo

One bill, three slices.

The mark is a circle split three ways, with a small gap between slices. Coral is you; the other two are friends. The wordmark is Sora Bold, tight.

on light ground
on dark ground
mark on coral: sit it on a white disc

Rules

  • Clear space: half the mark's width on every side.
  • Minimum size: mark 16px, wordmark 72px wide.
  • Placement: top-left of the host's home screen only. Inside the claim board the bill is the hero, not the logo.
  • Never recolour the slices, rotate the mark or add a shadow.

04
Claims

Your colour is always coral.

Dibs has no mascot. Its brand element is the claim: a coloured initial per friend, and a coral ring on anything you have called dibs on. You are coral on every screen; friends take indigo, violet and green.

ADKMFriends overlap by 8px. You (M) always come last and always in coral.

Fast. Honest. Interruptible.

Plain English, every time.

Say what happens, briefly and kindly. English only: no mixed-language copy anywhere in the product, including empty states and success messages.
