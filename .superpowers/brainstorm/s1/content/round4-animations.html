01
The rules

Four questions before anything moves.

Each of Dibs's animations has to pass all four. If it cannot say why it moves, it does not move.

1 · Should it animate at all?

Tap an item (tens per bill)Short: ≤160ms, ring and dot only
Press a buttonScale to 0.97, 140ms
Open the pay sheet (once per bill)Full: 320ms
Everyone's settled (rare)Delight: the one big moment
Keyboard moves a highlightNever

2 · Why does it move?

Feedback: the dot and ring confirm your tap.
Spatial: the sheet rises from where Pay lives.
State: the share amount changes, so it crossfades.
Jarring change: screens slide 24px so a swap is not a jump.

02
Easing

Strong out. Never in. Never bounce.

The three Dibs curves against two that are banned. Ease-in delays the first frames, which is exactly when the eye is watching. Bounce reads as childish and dated.

03
The tap

Calling dibs should feel like a click.

The signature moment. Tap an item: the row gets a coral ring, your dot arrives (95% size, fading in), a ripple confirms it, and your share changes with a short crossfade. Un-claiming is quieter and faster. Try it, then slow it down.

04
Buttons

Every button presses.

Scale to 0.97 on press in 140ms. Hover only where there is a real pointer. Focus gets a ring that appears instantly. Disabled does nothing.

Default · Secondary · Disabled. Tab to any of them to see the focus ring. Hover brightens by 6% over 150ms, gated to (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine).

05
Components

Sheet, dialog, toast, popover.

Each one enters from something, never from nothing. The sheet rises from the bottom edge, the popover grows from its trigger, the dialog stays centred, toasts stack.

06
Transitions

Screens slide a little. Themes swap whole.

Between screens: the old one drifts 16px and fades with 2px of blur while the new one arrives from 24px, in 280ms. Switching Light and Dark never animates every colour at once: it cross-fades the whole view in 240ms (View Transitions where supported, an instant swap elsewhere).

Theme switch

Use the Theme control in the top bar. In browsers with View Transitions the whole page cross-fades in 240ms; the colours never animate one by one, which would flash mismatched pairs. With Reduced motion on, it swaps instantly.

Hard rule: never put transition: all on the root.

07
Scroll

The host's list claims itself.

The only scroll-driven moment: on the host's review screen, rows pick up their ring one by one as you scroll through them (ScrollTrigger, scrubbed). Everything is readable before you scroll; the ring is the only thing that arrives.

09
Your call

Three things to approve.

1 · Overall feel

Crisp and quick: nothing over 320ms, strong ease-out everywhere, no bounce. Slower or softer is possible but I would not go past 400ms.

2 · The ripple

Only on claim, 280ms. It is the one decorative flourish. Say "quieter" to drop it to a ring-only confirm, or "more" to add a tiny dot pulse on the share amount.

3 · Theme switch

Whole-view cross-fade, 240ms, instant when Reduced motion is on. Alternative: always instant.

Reply "approved", or name what to change ("faster press", "no ripple", "softer sheet"). On approval, Step 5 builds every screen with exactly these tokens.
