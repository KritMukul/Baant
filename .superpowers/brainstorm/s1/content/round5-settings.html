Dibs · Step 5 · Screen 8 of 9 · Phone · Everyone

Two things to set. Nothing else.

Appearance for everyone, and the host's name and UPI ID so they do not retype them on every bill. Six states. In the light row the phone is set to light, in the dark row to dark, so you can see what "System" means and what happens when someone picks the other theme: the screen changes at once.

What this screen decides

  • Appearance is three cards, not a toggle. Light, Dark and System, each with a small picture of what it looks like. System is the default and it follows the phone live. The line under the cards says what System is right now ("It is Light right now"), because picking System changes nothing on screen and would otherwise look broken.
  • It applies immediately and is remembered on the phone. The build reads the choice before the first paint so there is no flash of the wrong theme. Arrow keys move through the three options the way a radio group does.
  • Remembered host means a name and a UPI ID, on this phone only. Home fills them in for the next bill. The field labels, the name@bank check and the messages are the same ones Home uses. Errors show after the field is left or Save is pressed, never while typing, and clear as soon as the value is fine.
  • Save says whether it is saved. It is disabled and reads "Saved" when nothing changed, "Save changes" when something did. Forgetting is behind a question that says what stays: bills already shared are not affected.
  • Honest about what Dibs cannot do. It checks the shape of a UPI ID, not that the account exists, and says "Check it twice". "How payments work" says that Dibs never holds money and that the host confirms each payment in the bank app.
  • Guests get the same Appearance, nothing else. They reach it from the board's menu. There is no host section for them.
  • Left out on purpose: notifications (there are none), language (English only), currency (INR only), an account (there is none).

Three things to approve

  • Appearance = Light, Dark, System as picture cards, System by default, applied at once and remembered on the phone.
  • Remembered host = name + UPI ID stored on this phone only, not an account. Your UPI ID goes to the server only when you share a bill, so that friends can pay you. "Forget" clears the phone and leaves shared bills alone.
  • Settings stays this small, with "How payments work" and "Privacy and terms" under About. The privacy and terms pages do not exist yet and must be written before a public launch.
