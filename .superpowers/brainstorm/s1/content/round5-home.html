Home. Scan the bill.

The first screen a host sees. Five states in light and dark, all live: add a photo, type a name and a UPI ID, press Scan. The in-app moon and sun button switches that phone's theme.

What this screen decides

  • One action per state. Empty: add a photo. Ready: Scan. Scanning: wait or cancel. Error: retake.
  • Remembered host. After the first bill, name and UPI ID are prefilled on this device ("Welcome back, Aarav"). Nothing leaves the device until you scan.
  • The disabled button explains itself in one line above it, so nobody wonders why it is grey.
  • UPI ID is validated as you type, but an error only shows after you leave the field.
  • Scanning is steps and skeleton rows shaped like the claim board, not a spinner. Cancel is always there.
  • Errors are rows, not illustrations. Plain reason, three tips, a retake button.
  • Tap targets are 44px or more, inputs are 50px, nothing is under 12px. All copy is English.

Three things to approve

  • Remember the host on this device (name and UPI ID in local storage). Recommended: it makes the second bill take one tap. The alternative is a blank form every time.
  • No "How it works" strip. The headline and subline say it in one sentence; a strip would push the form below the fold on small phones. Add it if you want it.
  • Gallery upload stays as a secondary choice next to the camera, for bills photographed earlier.
