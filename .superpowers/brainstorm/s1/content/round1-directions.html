Round 01 · Theme exploration

Baant, in five different worlds

Same screen in every card: the claim board, 8:54 pm, dinner at The Local Diners. Only palette, type and one signature move change, so you compare worlds and not layouts.

What it is

A bill splitter for friend groups in India. One person photographs the bill, everyone else taps what they ate on their own phone, then pays the host by UPI.

Who, and where

Four to eight friends at a table, mostly on Android, phone in one hand, slightly tipsy, in dim light. Guests never sign up, so trust has to come from the screen itself.

What it should feel like

Fair, fast and a little funny. Money is the one place the screen must be dead serious: the total and your share are always the largest numbers.

Critical screens

Claim board (the one people live in), host edit of scanned items, and the pay sheet that opens GPay or PhonePe with the amount filled in.
