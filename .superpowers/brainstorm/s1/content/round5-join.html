Dibs · Step 5 · Screen 7 of 9 · Phone · Guest

One field to join. No sign-up.

What a friend sees the first time they tap the link in WhatsApp. They learn who invited them and what the bill is, give a name, and land on the claim board. Seven states: the form (empty, filled, name taken, name missing, joining) and the two dead ends (a link that does not work, a bill that is already settled).

What this screen decides

  • The first line says why they are here. "Aarav invited you", then "Pick what you had." and one sentence of what happens next (tell us your name, tap your items, pay Aarav on UPI). A friend who has never heard of Dibs should not have to guess.
  • Enough of the bill to trust it, no more. Merchant, total, item and charge counts, who is hosting, who is already in. The item list appears once they have joined.
  • One field, no account. The name field asks for a first name and tells them why ("Friends see this next to your picks"). The input is built for phones: autocomplete=given-name, words capitalised, 24 characters, and the keyboard's key says "Go". Press it and they are in.
  • Mistakes are said in words, next to the field. An empty name gets one sentence. A name that is already on the bill asks "Is that you?" (people do open the link in a second browser and lose their cookie). The host's name cannot be used, so nobody can pose as the person being paid.
  • Dead ends say what happened and what to do. A link that does not match any bill is a different message from a bill that is already settled, and both offer the same way out: split your own bill.
  • Returning friends never see this screen. A cookie on their phone sends them straight to the board.

Three things to approve

  • Name only, no accounts. Joining is a first name and one tap. Returning guests on the same phone skip the screen.
  • "That's me" takes over an existing name. It is what a friend needs after switching browsers, and everyone at the table can see everyone, but nothing proves who is who. Payments are still confirmed by the host in the bank app, so the exposure is seeing someone's picks. The alternative is to always create "Mukul 2", which strands the first person's picks.
  • No theme toggle on this screen. Guests get light or dark from their phone. The toggle lives in Settings for the host and in the board's menu for everyone else.

Next after approval: Settings (theme toggle Light, Dark, System; remembered host). Then tablet and desktop.
