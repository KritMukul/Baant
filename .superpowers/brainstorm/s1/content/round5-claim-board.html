Claim board. Call dibs.

Where everyone spends their time. Six states in light and dark, all live: tap items to call dibs, watch your share move, and see what changes when friends tap at the same moment.

What this screen decides

  • The share is the second-biggest number after the bill total, always in the dock, always in mono.
  • Provisional versus final. Tax and service are spread over what has been claimed so far, so your share drops as others claim. Until every item has an owner the chip says Provisional and Pay is off, with the reason and a Nudge button.
  • Two people tap the same item: no conflict, it just splits. The newcomer's dot arrives, the row flashes once, and a toast says "Diya also called dibs on Surf N Turf. Split 2 ways."
  • The host gets leftovers handled: take them, or split them equally, then lock claims so every share becomes final.
  • Offline is a state, not an error: taps are kept and marked as waiting to sync.
  • Rows are 60px, item icons are drawn (no emoji), 8 real items from The Local Diners bill, ₹3,280.00.

Three things to approve

  • Pay stays off until everything is claimed. Safer (nobody overpays), but one idle friend can hold everyone up. The host's "take them" or "split equally" is the escape hatch. Alternative: allow paying early with a warning.
  • Quantity lines are one item. "Long Island Elec ×2" is claimed whole and shared equally between claimers. Claiming one of two units needs a stepper; I left it out to keep the tap simple.
  • Claiming has no undo timer. Tapping again releases. Fine for a table of friends; say so if you want a lock after paying.
