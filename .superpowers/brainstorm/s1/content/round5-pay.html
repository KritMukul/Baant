Dibs Pay Screen
Dibs · Step 5 · Screen 3 of 8 · Phone

Pay. And prove it.

Eight states, each one live and connected: press Pay, come back from the UPI app, add a UTR or a screenshot, send it to the host, and watch it go from Says paid to Confirmed. Starts on Light; use Show for Dark or Both.

What this screen decides

  • Dibs cannot see the payment, so the screen never says "paid" on its own. It says You say you paid, then Confirmed only when the host confirms in their bank app.
  • Coming back from the UPI app asks one question ("Did the payment go through?") instead of guessing.
  • Proof is optional and honest. A UTR (12 digits) and/or a screenshot. Dibs reads the screenshot and shows what it found: amount, payee, status, time. A mismatch offers the exact fix ("Pay the missing ₹900.00") and still lets you send anyway.
  • Reference code in the payment note (Dibs 7K2 Mukul) so the host can match the bank alert to the person.
  • Fallback when no UPI app opens (iPhone, no app installed): copy rows for ID, amount and note.
  • All copy is English; amounts are exact to the paisa; every tap target is 44px or more.

Three things to approve

  • "I already paid" is always available on the first state, for people who paid from another app or in cash. It skips the UPI hand-off and goes to proof.
  • A mismatch never blocks. It suggests the fix but lets the friend send anyway; the host decides. Blocking would trap people whose screenshot is simply unreadable.
  • The screenshot check is a helper, not a verdict. It catches honest mistakes. Someone can still fake an image; only the host's bank app is ground truth. The screen says so in one line.

Next, once approved: the host's Settle ledger (Unpaid / Says paid / Confirmed, with the Mark paid button), then Host fixes the scan, Share, Join, Settled, Settings.
