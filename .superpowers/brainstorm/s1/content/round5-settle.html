Settle up. Who has paid?

The host's ledger. Every friend is Unpaid, Says paid or Confirmed, and only the host can move someone to Confirmed. Six live states: tap a row to open it, confirm a payment, mark one paid in cash, remind the others.

What this screen decides

  • The host is the source of truth. A friend can say they paid; only the host's tap makes it Confirmed. The progress bar shows three layers: confirmed (green), says paid (accent), unpaid (grey).
  • Confirming is a checklist, not a guess. The row opens to the friend's UTR and screenshot result, and tells the host exactly what to look for in their bank app: the amount and the reference code in the note (Dibs 7K2 Kabir).
  • A short payment is its own state. If the screenshot shows less than owed, the row says so in warning colour and offers "Ask for ₹900.00 more", "Confirm anyway" and "Not received".
  • Cash and other ways are allowed. "Mark paid" asks once ("Mark Diya as paid? Use this if they paid you another way.") and records that you did it.
  • Amounts are exact. They come from the real split engine: Aarav ₹707.71, Diya ₹733.13, Kabir ₹839.07, Mukul ₹1,000.09, total ₹3,280.00.
  • The bill is Settled only when every friend is Confirmed. That is the one place the single emoji appears.

Three things to approve

  • Reminders are a message you send, not a push. "Remind" opens the phone's share sheet with a prewritten line and the link; Dibs has no friends' phone numbers and no notifications while there are no accounts.
  • "Confirm anyway" exists for short payments. Real life: a friend pays the rest in cash later. It records that you accepted a short amount.
  • Un-confirming is allowed from a small "Undo" in the confirmed row, for a mis-tap. It does not delete anything, it moves the friend back to Says paid.
